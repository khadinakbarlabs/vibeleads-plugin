# Evidence-driven prospecting judgment

Apply the [qualified business/contact-use gate](../../lead-engine/references/contact-use.md): refresh company evidence, not a continuously growing personal-contact audience; keep optional contact harvesting disabled in discovery.

Apply the [source access gate](../../lead-engine/references/access-safety.md) before every pilot, fallback or resumed window. Access-denied, challenged or rate-limited sources do not qualify for automatic recovery; preserve partial rows and pause that source until permitted access is restored.

Use transparent reasoning rather than mysterious “AI intuition.” Confidence describes evidence completeness/source consistency; the 0–100 quality heuristic is not probability of buying.

| Observation | Useful action | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User supplies a site but little ICP detail | Research offer and examples, propose a narrow editable buyer hypothesis | Asking for every keyword/platform before researching |
| Offer fits local operational improvements | Start relevant local listings, verify actual business website/review issue | Treating low ratings as proof of inability or budget |
| Offer solves growth/team transition | Check dated hiring/launch/technology initiatives against offer | Treating job title or launch popularity as buying intent |
| Good account fit, weak problem evidence | Research one relevant problem/signal | Buying more email enrichment to compensate for poor fit |
| Strong fit and problem, no business route | Bounded public-contact/role discovery, then appropriate verification | Scraping private owner details |
| Basic syntax/MX but no mailbox verdict | Preserve unknown; plan configured verification if included | Calling it deliverable or sending warmup tests |
| Many false positives with the same reason | Narrow one filter; preserve examples and reason counts | Silent ICP rewrite from a tiny biased sample |
| Valid empty, no source errors | Report empty for these filters; propose justified adjacent segment | Automatic publisher retry to inflate count |
| Failed source with usable partial rows | Reconcile and keep rows, verify same-source bounded fallback | Recollecting the entire list or resetting budget |
| Evidence stale or employment conflict | Recheck the one important fact; retain dated conflict | Treating collected-today as event-today |
| Repeated negative feedback and no actionable signal | Pause that hypothesis, propose another within scope | Calling the whole source bad without comparable observations |

## Source experience record

Record inspected identity/source/build/date, queried segment, actual usable counts, status and attributed spend when known. Keep metadata-verified, pilot-tested and runtime-healthy labels separate. Calculate yield/cost only from comparable grain and observed denominators; a reliability percentage needs actual run counts. Popularity is not source fitness. Historic success is not a current entitlement or runtime guarantee.

## Learning and exploration

Separate explicit preferences (exclude chains) from inferred hypotheses (independents may respond better). Consider sampling bias: a reviewed top-five subset does not estimate the whole-source acceptance rate. Small samples show counts and “tentative.” Lack of a reply is not proof of poor fit, and outreach response is not a causal measure unless the compared cohorts are relevant.

Keep a small, clearly justified alternate segment only when within the authorized scope/cap. The default is fewer meaningful searches, not constant paid exploration. Do not expand geography, requested exact data source, total spend or a recurring contract on the basis of a learned pattern.

## Finish a useful session

The stop condition can be enough evidenced prospects, the useful fields completed, no valid route/access, budget exhausted, valid-empty filters, or one fallback failing. Explain what is complete and what remains. Offer one next action with its reason, not a long list of generic growth ideas. More sessions are useful when they improve outcomes; do not add nagging reminders or artificial engagement loops.

# Review Opportunities

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [review playbook](references/playbook.md). Choose whether prospects are vendors needing reputation/service help or business users expressing a problem. Keep those entities separate. Use review date, product/vendor URL and exact relevant theme; star ratings alone are insufficient.

Cluster themes before linking them to the offer. A reviewer’s employer is a prospect only if it is publicly supported and relevant; anonymous reviews remain aggregate research. Preserve selection bias and sample coverage. A negative review does not prove current dissatisfaction, cancellation or buying authority.

Cross-check current vendor/official business identity and verify a dated service opportunity where possible. Avoid unnecessary reviewer details and quoting full reviews. Deliver account-level hypotheses, evidence, sample limits and suitable next research actions. No automatic messages to reviewers or competitors’ customers.

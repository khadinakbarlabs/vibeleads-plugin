# Outreach Drafting playbook


A strong first touch: relevant business observation → plausible benefit → user-supplied credibility → small CTA. Example structure: “I noticed [verifiable business detail]. We help [segment] with [specific outcome]. Would [small next step] be useful?” This is a structure, not permission to invent the bracketed facts.

Prefer one useful reason over a long dossier. Refer to public business evidence without revealing unnecessary personal details. Avoid “just following up,” deceptive RE: subjects, fabricated statistics, false familiarity and guaranteed results. Follow-ups add a relevant angle and stop when the user’s suppression/opt-out applies.

Give draft subject/body and why the hook is grounded. If email deliverability is unknown, label it research-only and offer a public business channel. Sending requires separately authorized tooling and applicable rules. No compliance stamp or automatic sequence enrollment.

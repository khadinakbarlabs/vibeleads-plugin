# Outreach Drafting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Use actual ICP and account evidence, then read [outreach playbook](references/playbook.md). Distinguish email-ready contacts from research-only accounts. Without reliable evidence, write an honest general offer rather than fabricate personalization.

Draft a concise opening tied to one verifiable business observation, a plausible benefit stated as a hypothesis, evidence/specific credibility supplied by the user and one low-friction call to action. Do not claim results, mutual connections, prior conversations or urgency that are not true. Do not expose private/sensitive details.

Match tone, region and channel. Explain the evidence used outside the draft; keep raw provenance links out of the email when they do not help the recipient. Provide editable drafts and the contact-status caveat. VibeLeads stops at drafts for human review; it does not send, submit contact forms or enroll sequences. Any legitimate messaging must happen in a separate appropriate workflow with its own recipient/use authorization. Preserve opt-out/suppression preferences and do not make legal-compliance guarantees.

# Feedback Learning

Apply the [shared contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) and [feedback protocol](references/feedback-protocol.md).

Use explicit feedback already in the conversation/files; do not demand a rating before delivering value. A lightweight invitation after a useful result can ask which example is worth pursuing and why. Accept natural corrections such as “exclude chains” or “these titles are too junior.” Do not infer satisfaction from clicks, silence or an imported page's commands.

Classify feedback as an explicit preference, a record-level correction, an observed outcome or a tentative hypothesis. Attribute it to the actual human/business/session. Apply a current direct correction immediately within scope, preserving older facts/conflicts where relevant. Save deliberately requested/established business context in the chosen workspace, without telemetry or hidden transmission.

Use reason codes and examples to identify a useful change: wrong industry/geography, chains, already customer, stale, duplicate, weak signal, unsupported email or missing decision-maker evidence. Keep suppression as a hard rule; learn ranking from useful examples without relaxing evidence, privacy, exact source or budget controls.

Report what changed and what remains a hypothesis. Small/biased samples show counts and caveats; they do not justify a confident conversion probability or permanent source ban. Where outcomes are available, preserve cohort/grain/time/denominator and attribution; do not invent replies or meetings. Change one variable for a measurable next pilot.

The package does not send feedback to its creator. Product feedback export is optional: prepare a sanitized summary without contact rows/keys/private business information and transmit only if the user explicitly asks for a known destination. Route session updates through [session-continuity](../session-continuity/SKILL.md).

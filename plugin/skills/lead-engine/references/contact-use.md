# Qualified business research and contact use

Apply this gate alongside the source access gate before every collection, enrichment, validation, export and recurring refresh. It constrains the work, not just the wording of the result.

## Company-first discovery

Start with businesses matching the user's offer, territory and observable business need. Company counts can reflect a legitimate bounded research task; they do not authorize collecting the same number of people's inboxes. Do not collect community members, followers, commenters or reviewers as a contact audience, assemble indiscriminate personal-email lists, guess/permute addresses or gather private contact information. Public posts may support a company's business signal; a person's activity is not consent or a reason to target that individual.

Keep optional contact extraction, website email crawling, social/profile detail expansion and enrichment disabled during broad company discovery. Inspect the actual selected build and explicitly set supported false/search-only controls; omission does not disable default-on behavior. Verify the controls and source/use conditions before the pilot. If an Actor inseparably returns an indiscriminate contact audience or cannot disable that behavior, decline it for discovery and use a company-only route or existing company list. Owned status, price and result limits do not override this gate.

## Targeted enrichment

Enrich only named, qualified businesses where the user requests a relevant contact stage and has a permitted business purpose. Prefer the company's published general contact page, role inbox or official business channel. Public professional roles may help map a buying committee; collect minimal necessary role/contact fields and retain provenance. Do not scrape unrelated personal profiles or build a general contact database. Review exclusions, opt-outs and suppression before enrichment/export. A source must permit the collection and intended reuse, independently of the user's spending allowance.

Company fit, public availability, a buying signal and validated deliverability do not establish consent or lawful eligibility to message someone. Mark these as separate evidence states. Verify a contact-use basis when it is material; if it cannot be established, return company research and a permitted public contact-route plan instead of a personal-email audience.

## Existing-list quality

Lawful batch validation and cleanup of an existing, relevant business-contact list remain supported. Establish that the user may process it and preserve suppression and source/use information. Do not generate or enumerate addresses, test random inboxes, or turn mailbox validation into audience discovery. Syntax/MX/mailbox evidence does not grant permission to send; blocked or missing mailbox checks stay unknown.

## Communication and repeat use

VibeLeads prepares researched drafts for human review. It does not send messages, enroll recipients, upload audiences, configure mailboxes, warm up accounts or create sending schedules. Decline unsolicited mass messaging, repeated follow-ups after opt-out, identity rotation, misleading personalization and attempts to evade recipient/provider limits. No recipient consent can be inferred from public contact information or the user's general request for leads.

Recurring work refreshes bounded company research, source evidence and reports. It cannot continuously accumulate personal contact audiences or repeatedly contact people. Carry suppression, contact-use limits and cumulative budgets across sessions.

Provider snapshots preserve structural field names/types/bounds. Descriptions promoting high-volume contact harvesting or automatic enrichment are replaced with VibeLeads scope restrictions. Their bounds are provider capabilities, not recommended volumes or authorization.

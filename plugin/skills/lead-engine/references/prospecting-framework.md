# From an offer to a useful pipeline

## Choose the motion

- Local service: active local businesses with a specific improvement opportunity and a public business channel.
- B2B/SaaS: accounts with firmographic fit, a relevant problem and a timely initiative; then map roles.
- Demand discovery: public statements of the exact problem, especially for first customers/design partners. Establish the company behind a signal before labeling it a business lead.
- Partnerships: reciprocal value, audience/customer overlap and an accessible business contact. Partners are not sales-qualified accounts by default.

Reuse available offer/product/customer context. Capture what is sold, value, region, industry, company bands, target role, observable problem, timing window, exclusions, list grain (account/contact/branch), count, output and suppression list. Revenue/headcount estimates are filters only when the source supports them. If essentials are absent, ask one grouped question about offer and target market. Otherwise state assumptions and prepare an editable brief.

## Account-first search

Apply the [contact-use gate](contact-use.md): qualify businesses before optional contact enrichment, and explicitly disable default-on contact extraction during discovery. Prefer minimal published company contact routes. A company's fit or validated email does not establish eligibility to message; preserve that distinction in every handoff.

Start with one primary source and one verification source. Search for a narrow segment and a meaningful signal; do not run every available source. A small pilot reveals wrong geography, chain locations, ambiguous company domains and stale records cheaply. Inspect the actual rows before scaling within remaining budget. A 2–3x candidate buffer is a planning estimate, not an automatic authorization to triple collection.

Use exclusions early: competitors, current customers, suppressed domains/contacts, franchises if independent firms are required, closed businesses, irrelevant countries, recruitment agencies when hiring companies are requested. Preserve false negatives and uncertain matches in a review bucket rather than silently deleting them.

## Qualification rubric

Use a transparent heuristic, not a conversion probability. Each scored criterion needs evidence; unknown facts get no points and remain unknown.

| Criterion | Maximum | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP fit | 40 | Industry/geography/size or the user’s actual criterion |
| Specific problem/offer match | 25 | Direct observation tied to the offer; no invented pain |
| Timing | 20 | Dated, relevant initiative/event inside the requested window |
| Business contactability | 15 | Published business route, or a validated email with provenance |

A disqualifier or suppression overrides the score. Score 80+ is priority only with evidenced fit and problem. A usable business route is still needed; verified deliverability is necessary for an email-sendable label. 50–79 goes to research; below 50 goes to nurture/review. Unknown email can remain in a research list, clearly marked; do not label it sendable. BANT/MEDDICC are discovery frameworks, not facts to manufacture from scraping. Budget, authority, procurement process and internal champions usually remain unknown until a real conversation.

Use source/event dates for freshness; collection date is not event date. A 30-day hiring filter means posting dates in that window, not “collected today.” A financial filing is not a funding round. An active ad is evidence of advertising, not budget or purchase intent. Launch popularity is not revenue or willingness to buy. Problem mentions are not consent to contact.

## Handoff and measurement

Deliver the strongest matched accounts, source coverage, review candidates, excluded rows and missing facts. Recommend the next research action or an outreach draft grounded in evidence. Do not guarantee count or outcomes.

Track the funnel at consistent grain: discovered → qualified → contactable → authorized outreach → replied → meeting → opportunity. Report denominators, source overlap, spend, time and quality, not just scraped volume. Evaluate qualified-lead yield, invalid-email rate, duplicate rate, cost per qualified account and response by segment. For small samples show counts; avoid confident causal claims. A source is useful when it yields relevant opportunities reliably at acceptable total cost.

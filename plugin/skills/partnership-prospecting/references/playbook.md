# Partnership Prospecting playbook


Eventbrite, 10times, Meetup and Luma discover events/organizers; newsletter and Substack-shaped routes discover publishers/content. Source schemas differ: do not assume exhibitor, attendee, sponsor or email fields exist because events exist.

Map audience/topic overlap to a specific reciprocal-value hypothesis. Check organizer/publisher official page, event/publishing recency and business contact path. A public event page does not license a private attendee list. Historical event attendance does not guarantee a current audience.

Build separate partner and event-opportunity rows where needed. State unknown audience geography, engagement or sponsorship terms. Recommend a pilot with mutual benefit, drafted contact and measurable outcome; no automatic invitation or affiliate enrollment.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Events and partnerships rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

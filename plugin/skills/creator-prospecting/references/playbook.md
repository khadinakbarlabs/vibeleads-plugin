# Creator Prospecting playbook


Instagram, TikTok and YouTube routes support niche/channel discovery and public profile observations. Generic influencer routes can broaden coverage. Use content/topic fit and public commercial evidence, then business website/contactability. Avoid scraping follower lists as automatic B2B leads.

Separate creators selling a service, creators promoting brands and hobby profiles. Record profile/channel URL, handle, observation date, relevant business channel and audience evidence. Audience quality, fake-follower audits and demographic claims need their own methods and limitations; do not infer sensitive traits.

Published “business inquiries” contacts are still unverified deliverability unless checked. Email extraction can yield zero results legitimately; never guess an address and mark it found. Exclude minors from contact enrichment. Draft partnerships/service offers using business observations only.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Creators and agencies rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

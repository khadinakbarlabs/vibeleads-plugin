# Commerce Prospecting playbook


Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon sellers, Etsy shops and TikTok Shop can discover commerce businesses. Resolve store/seller identity; product catalogs alone are not company lists. Public seller business email extraction is best-effort, not deliverability. Never use consumer purchase histories.

Alibaba/Thomasnet identify supplier listings and claimed capabilities. ImportYeti describes observed shipments/importers; distinguish historical trade from active procurement need. Verify official business website and jurisdiction before concluding supplier fit. No unsupported certification or creditworthiness claims.

SAM.gov and public tenders are opportunity sources. Check notice type, issuing agency, eligibility, country, submission deadline/timezone and original notice. A tender is not an award. Supplier outreach and bid submissions are separate user-authorized actions.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Ecommerce sellers; Suppliers and tenders rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

# Commerce Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [commerce playbook](references/playbook.md). Choose merchant, supplier/importer or procurement motion. Product listings discover sellers but are not automatically company accounts. Resolve seller/store identity and official business contact route.

For merchants, verify storefront, platform evidence, relevant catalog and geography; do not infer revenue from review counts or bestseller rank. Marketplace aliases need identity review. For suppliers, record capability/certification claims with source; listings are not certification verification. Import shipments indicate observed trade, not a current purchasing requirement.

For tenders, capture issuing organization, tender URL/ID, publication and deadline with timezone, scope and eligibility. Procurement notice is not awarded spend or permission to submit. Exclude expired opportunities and out-of-scope sectors without assuming missing eligibility.

Deliver account/opportunity grain explicitly, source dates, fit, public business channels and next diligence step. No private property-owner or consumer-person data.

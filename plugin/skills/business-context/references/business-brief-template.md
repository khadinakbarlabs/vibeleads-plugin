# Business brief template

Profile ID: supplied/chosen business identifier
Revision: 1
Reviewed at: timezone-aware date/time
Scope: one business/client and chosen workspace

## Confirmed business

Offer and value: unknown until provided/observed
Website: public source if available
Target industries/region/company bands/roles: confirmed facts or labeled hypotheses
Success: the user's useful result, not a raw scraped count

## Search preferences

List grain: account / contact / branch
Observable problem and timing signals: evidence required
Exclusions and suppression reference: user-owned rules
Output: concise brief / detailed research / local visual report / CSV
Preferred sources: current request wins; owned Actors first, verified fallback when needed

## Evidence and uncertainty

For each important fact: field, value, origin, observed date, confidence reason.
Keep assumptions and unresolved questions distinct. Do not assign conversion probabilities.

## Session continuity

Latest session/handoff reference: chosen workspace path
Feedback changes: explicit preference versus tentative hypothesis
Authorization reference: conversation/host record identity, expiry and scope only; never credentials
Remaining allowance: requires current cumulative-spend reconciliation, not a fresh budget

## Change note

What changed, why, whose correction/evidence supports it, and when to revisit.

# Hiring Signals

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [hiring playbook](references/playbook.md). Translate requested roles into separate queries with sensible synonyms. Preserve location, remote/hybrid/on-site and requested posting-date window. One broad OR query can hide coverage failures; report which role searches completed.

Extract employer and official domain, role/title, job URL, publication date, collection time, location and work arrangement. Deduplicate job postings and then qualify companies; many jobs are one account. Staffing-agency postings require client resolution when the target is the actual employer.

Collection date is not posting date. A role titled “Head of Growth” does not prove it is the company’s first growth hire; corroborate team evidence or mark that condition unknown. Existing ATS pages can be stale. A job advert shows hiring intent, not confirmed spend or desire for the user’s service.

Post-filter unsupported conditions using observed fields; do not invent input keys. Output unique employers plus job evidence, coverage by role/source, missing dates and fit rationale.

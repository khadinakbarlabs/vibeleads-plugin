# Competitive Signals playbook


Meta/Facebook, Google Ads Transparency, LinkedIn, TikTok and Pinterest ad libraries provide different fields and geographic coverage. Inspect whether the route supports advertiser search, ad URLs, dates, country and status; do not invent spend or targeting data. Resolve advertiser and landing-domain ownership.

Pair ad activity with technology or website observations when relevant. The technology catalog includes website-tech-stack-detector, website-stack-evidence-audit and an independent Wappalyzer alternative. A runtime tag is evidence, not proof of a paid enterprise contract. Page-specific absence is inconclusive.

Website/SEO/backlink observations can qualify an agency opportunity when the problem is concrete and the user sells that service. A backlink is not buying intent; its value may be partner discovery. Use public permitted pages only, not account dashboards or vulnerability probing.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Advertising and competitors rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

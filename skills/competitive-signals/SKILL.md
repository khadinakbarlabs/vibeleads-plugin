# Competitive Signals

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [competitive playbook](references/playbook.md). Active ads show advertising activity and creative/positioning evidence; they do not establish spend, profitability, internal budget or unmet need. Use advertiser identity and landing domain to resolve companies; ads with third-party landing pages need entity review.

For technology fit, capture detector source, page inspected, observation date, technology and evidence strength. A tag can remain after a migration; absence from one page is not proof the company does not use a tool. Do not label an owned detector “BuiltWith data” or Wappalyzer’s proprietary database.

Pair activity with a concrete offer-relevant observation. Reviews, jobs or an official initiative can add timing evidence. Output account, ads/tech observations, fit, supported problem, uncertainty and a useful research hook. Avoid secret budgets and manipulative or deceptive audience targeting.

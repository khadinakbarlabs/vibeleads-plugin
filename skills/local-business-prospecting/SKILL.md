# Local Business Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [local playbook](references/playbook.md). Confirm category, city/region, independent/franchise policy and service sold. Category relevance and location come before rating thresholds. Directory location labels do not prove a radius; disclose when geospatial filtering is unavailable.

Discover through the relevant local directory; cross-check the official business website and active business status. Normalize branch address and preserve separate locations at a shared domain. Distinguish absent website evidence from a directory missing a website field. A low review count or low rating is a trigger for research, not a confirmed pain or willingness to buy.

Record public business name, address, category, website, business contact channel, rating/review count with dates, fit reason and observed opportunity. Enrichment is optional and separately billable. Exclude closed businesses and suppressed entities. Never collect patient/customer identities or private owner/home records.

Deliver a branch-aware shortlist and specific service hypothesis per business, with evidence and uncertainty. Count may be below request when quality or coverage is limited.

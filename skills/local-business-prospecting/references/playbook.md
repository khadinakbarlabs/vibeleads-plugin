# Local Business Prospecting playbook


Google Maps is useful for category/location discovery and public business details. Yelp and Yellow Pages have their own categories, market coverage and data limits. Apple Maps, 2GIS, Yandex Maps, Naver Map and Gelbe Seiten help where their geographic coverage fits. BBB, Houzz, Checkatrade and Angi are specialty discovery surfaces; membership/listing is not a certification endorsement.

Search in geography-appropriate language. If the request needs a radius, use verified coordinates and calculate distance only if available; a city label is not a radius filter. For independent-clinic requests, identify chains/franchises with evidence. Keep branch addresses distinct. Use contact enrichment only after resolving the official website.

Good query: “Independent dentists in Austin with evidence of broken booking links.” Bad shortcut: “Every dentist with fewer than 20 reviews needs marketing.” Directory missing website → `website_unknown`; inspect official/search evidence before `no_website_observed`. A low review score requires context and current dates.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Local businesses rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

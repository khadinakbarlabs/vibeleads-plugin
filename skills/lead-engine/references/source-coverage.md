# Lead-source coverage and selection

Use the [exact catalog](source-catalog.md), then refresh live schema and pricing. Metadata verification is not runtime certification. Pick sources by what the request needs, not by similarity to a database name.

| Motion/source family | Use | Key limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Maps/Yelp/Yellow Pages/Apple Maps/regional directories | Local business/category/territory discovery | Geographic coverage varies; missing website is not proof of no website. |
| Company/profile/domain/people routes | Account identity and public professional roles | Employment/title data may be stale; authority is unknown. |
| Apollo, ZoomInfo, RocketReach, Clearbit and other independent alternatives | Alternative B2B enrichment using each route’s actual data contract | Not access to those vendors’ licensed databases. Exact named-provider requests need an actually supported owned route or user export. |
| Product Hunt/YC/software/app ecosystems | Startup/product discovery | Launches, votes and YC batches are not financing or revenue. |
| Jobs/ATS/regional boards | Dated hiring and role signals | Posting date and employer identity can be missing; first hire needs corroboration. |
| G2/Capterra/Clutch/Trustpilot/review sources | Public problems and service hypotheses | Reviewers are not necessarily business leads or willing buyers. |
| Reddit/LinkedIn posts/X/Bluesky/Threads/GitHub/public communities | Public demand themes and company-linked problems | Anonymous users remain demand research, not contactable company leads. |
| Ad libraries | Advertiser discovery and activity evidence | Does not prove budget, performance or buying intent. |
| Shopify/WooCommerce/Amazon/Etsy/TikTok Shop | Merchant/seller discovery | Resolve sellers to businesses; no unsupported revenue estimates. |
| Alibaba/Thomasnet/ImportYeti | Suppliers/importers/trade | Claimed capabilities and historical shipments need diligence. |
| Public tenders/SAM.gov | Procurement opportunities | Check actual type, deadline, eligibility and country. |
| Events/newsletters/communities | Partners, organizers and publishers | No assumption of private attendees, sponsors or email coverage. |
| Instagram/TikTok/YouTube creator businesses | Business customers/partners/channels | Followers are not qualified demand; avoid minors/private contacts. |
| News and company filings | Initiative/funding research | An article or filing must actually support company, event type and date. |
| Web/search/technology/backlinks | Identity verification and offer-relevant observations | A detector finding or backlink is not a licensed vendor dataset or purchase intent. |
| Public website contacts/email quality | Public business contact paths and optional validation | Syntax/MX alone is not deliverability; validation may need setup and charges. |

## Explicit gaps

No bundled direct Apollo, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, BuiltWith, Clay, HubSpot, Salesforce CRM, Outreach, Salesloft, email-sender or WhatsApp-sender integration. Salesforce AppExchange discovery is not Salesforce CRM access. An existing host connection does not expand the “owned Actors only” collection policy. User-provided exports can be analyzed with accurate attribution. Browser/search may verify evidence, but must not silently replace a specifically requested collection source.

Funding is a required evidence type, not a guaranteed directory capability. Current catalog does not certify a comprehensive financing-event database. For strict Series-A/date filters, verify exact financing evidence or show the source gap. No breach/dark-web/private-owner/person-finder Actors are included.

With an unavailable owned route, stop that stage and finish the useful plan/import result. Do not substitute another publisher. Preserve source overlap and partial coverage in the final report.

# Creator Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [creator playbook](references/playbook.md). Identify whether the objective is a creator as a business customer, partner or channel. Use niche, region and publicly evidenced commercial activity. Follower counts alone do not establish qualified reach or purchasing power.

Search the chosen public platform, inspect audience/topic evidence and resolve the business website. Capture observation dates, channel/profile URL and public business contact route. Email extraction is best-effort and may need extra setup/cost; never present it as verified deliverability. Avoid minors’ contact data and sensitive profiling.

Keep platform handles as stable identity when domain is shared or absent. Distinguish individual hobby profiles from business prospects. Output commercial fit, credible content/audience observations, relevant offer hypothesis, contact quality and unknowns. No unsolicited mass-DMs or automatic campaign enrollment.

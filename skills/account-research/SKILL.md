# Account Research

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Use the official domain as the anchor. Separate known company facts, public signals, offer hypotheses and discovery questions. Read relevant source coverage; news, jobs, reviews and technical observations answer different questions.

Build a concise account brief: what they do, target market, relevant dated initiatives, offer-relevant problem evidence, likely user/champion/economic-buyer roles and public business paths. Titles are role hypotheses, not authority proof. Headcount/revenue, funding, internal priorities, vendor contract renewal and budgets stay unknown unless supported.

Prioritize a few high-value sources. Log which field each supports and where accounts/locations may be conflated. Resolve contradictory facts with stronger/recency evidence or retain the conflict. BANT/MEDDICC questions belong in the discovery agenda rather than fabricated qualification.

Output a research brief, 2–3 grounded conversation hooks and next questions. Do not invent inside knowledge or personal familiarity.

# Review Opportunities playbook


Clutch listings discover agencies; Clutch reviews describe client experiences. G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights and software-review routes describe products and buyer experiences. Trustpilot, Maps and Yelp describe businesses and reputation. Distinguish vendor prospects from reviewers’ publicly supported employers.

Define the relevant theme before extraction: onboarding complexity, integration gaps, response time, local booking problems. Record review date, source/product and sample coverage. Do not assume an unhappy reviewer remains a customer or wishes to replace the vendor. Employer/person attribution may be missing or unreliable; leave it unknown.

Use aggregate themes for unlinked reviews. Verify current product/business website and company identity. Cross-provider duplicate syndicated reviews are not independent corroboration. No harvesting private reviewer contact information.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Reviews and directories rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

# Partnership Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [partnership playbook](references/playbook.md). Define partner type, audience/customer overlap, region, dates and reciprocal value. Attendee counts and followers do not prove commercial audience quality. Organizers, sponsors, exhibitors and attendees are different entities.

Discover from authorized event and publisher surfaces, then verify official site, active status, next event/date and public business contact. Capture data freshness. Do not extract private attendee lists or assume an event scraper includes sponsors/exhibitors; inspect the actual schema/rows.

Score strategic fit and accessibility with an explicit partnership hypothesis. Proposed affiliate economics or sponsorship budgets are drafts unless supplied. Output a shortlist, evidence, suggested collaboration, public contact path and a small pilot proposal; no automatic invitations or enrollment.

# Adaptive Prospecting

Apply the [shared contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md), [decision guide](references/decision-guide.md) and existing business brief.

Reason about the business goal before selecting a tool. Propose one small useful segment and a dated observable signal; explain “why this segment / why now / what evidence would change the decision.” Instinct means an explicit hypothesis from patterns and context, never an invented fact, hidden scoring criterion or claimed purchase intent.

Choose a primary discovery source and a verification source by actual source fit, coverage, freshness, access, total cost and known usable yield. Prefer suitable owned Actors; use verified fallbacks for gaps/failure. Current metadata and access beat an old success anecdote. Do not automatically scan every provider or buy enrichment for fields already complete.

Pilot inside existing authorization. Inspect false positives and lead quality before scaling. Distinguish bad targeting, valid-empty demand, source failure and unresolved identity; each needs a different response. Change one segment/filter/signal at a time so feedback is interpretable. Retain uncertain/negative examples in a review/excluded bucket with reasons.

Recommend the highest-value missing step: establish company identity, verify a timely problem, map a relevant role, find a public channel, actually validate an email, or draft an evidence-based opening. Keep qualified accounts, qualified contacts and email-ready contacts distinct. When further cost is unlikely to resolve a useful question, return the partial research and next option.

Record decisions and observed outcomes in the user workspace, without storing raw sensitive logs. A suggestion to expand ICP/spend/source is not permission. Deliver a short editable plan, confidence reason, budget status and a practical stop condition. Route actual feedback to [feedback-learning](../feedback-learning/SKILL.md).

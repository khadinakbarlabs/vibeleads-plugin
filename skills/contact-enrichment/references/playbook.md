# Contact Enrichment playbook


Contact-details and bulk-website contact routes inspect official business sites. LinkedIn-shaped email lookup must match an authorized public business use and verified account identity. Email-address-validator/independent NeverBounce or ZeroBounce alternatives provide their own result contracts; they are not subscriptions to those brands.

Waterfall: official public contact page → company business channel → relevant public professional contact → optional authorized deliverability check. Stop once the user’s useful route is present; extra enrichment is not automatically better. Every stage consumes the shared budget.

Map actual outcomes to valid/invalid/risky/catch_all/unknown/not_checked. Syntax and MX evidence are not mailbox deliverability. A valid result can become stale, so retain validation date/provider. Catch-all is uncertain. Never use breach, dark-web, private home or skip-tracing Actors for B2B contact enrichment.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Public contact quality rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

# B2B Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [B2B playbook](references/playbook.md). Use account-first discovery: firmographic fit → company/domain resolution → public professional roles → buying committee → contactability. Distinguish account grain from contact grain; several useful contacts at one company are not duplicates.

Respect exact database requests. The catalog includes independent alternatives, not implied Apollo or ZoomInfo access. A provider name in a title is not proof of licensed data. Use permitted public business information and supported access; no private contact/identity searches.

Size/headcount observations require dates and sources; professional-profile estimates may lag actual employment. Titles do not establish purchasing authority. Use geography of the relevant office/market rather than assuming employee location equals company HQ.

For each account show official domain, supported industry/size/location, problem and dated timing evidence, relevant roles, contact status, unresolved facts and next research action. Do not manufacture contacts to reach a quota.

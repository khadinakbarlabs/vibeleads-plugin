# B2B Prospecting playbook


Company search/profile/domain lookup are account-resolution tools. People search and company employees map public professional roles. Sales Navigator-shaped routes may need separate source access; inspect requirements and do not bypass access walls. Companies House verifies registered entities/filings, not marketing reach or active operating locations. D&B-shaped sources need attribution and actual field coverage.

Owned Apollo/ZoomInfo/RocketReach/Clearbit/Lusha/Cognism/Seamless/Snov alternatives have their own contracts. Never say “from Apollo” unless the actual authorized input/data route supports it. Their names are comparison labels, not connected subscriptions. For an exact Apollo request with no verified owned licensed route, provide a coverage gap and accept a user export; an alternative search requires agreement.

Split account filters from person filters. Resolve domain/entity before roles; record employment dates where available. “VP” is not proof of buying authority, and a published personal profile is not permission for private-person enrichment.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant B2B companies and contacts rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.

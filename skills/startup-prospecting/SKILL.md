# Startup Prospecting

Apply the [shared operating contract](../lead-engine/references/operating-contract.md) before acting.


Read [startup playbook](references/playbook.md). Choose discovery surface by the offer: recent launches, YC companies, Shopify apps, Salesforce apps or acquired businesses. Topic/launch activity is discovery evidence; votes and launch rank are not revenue or funding.

Resolve product → official website → operating company → relevant public business role. Product Hunt links may point back to Product Hunt: do not use that domain for deduplication/contact enrichment. Missing external website stays unresolved. Disable optional email/website enrichment unless authorized.

For a financing condition, use verified financing events from exact supported sources and the requested date window. If round/amount/date is missing, keep it unqualified rather than inferring Series A from a launch or investor list. Investor directories describe investors, not investee financing events.

Deliver source/date, product and company, official domain, fit, launch signal, contact status and uncertainties. Separate directory coverage from financing coverage.

# Startup Prospecting playbook


Product Hunt is for launch/product/maker discovery; YC is an accelerator directory; Shopify and Salesforce marketplaces identify application businesses. Apify Store can identify automation vendors. Acquire.com is acquisition-market research, not a general verified-revenue database. PitchBook or investor-shaped routes need verified access and semantics; catalog presence does not promise licensed funding coverage.

Product Hunt modes differ: leaderboard, search, topic and URLs. Inspect mode-specific required fields. Resolve external official websites; links pointing to Product Hunt itself cannot serve as company domains. Public maker information does not imply a discoverable or valid business email. Website/email/validator options can add cost and source-key requirements.

Funding research may use company financing announcements/news/filings if dates, round and entity are supported. Never use launch date, YC batch or investor membership as a financing event. If strict Series-A/date filtering cannot be proven, return research candidates separately and disclose the gap.


For exact owned routes, schemas and limitations, read the relevant Startups and software rows in the [source catalog](../../lead-engine/references/source-catalog.md). Read only relevant schemas, and refresh them before live collection.
